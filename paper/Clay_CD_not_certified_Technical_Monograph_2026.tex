\begin{abstract}
A short Lean~4 illustration (\texttt{lean/NotBlowup.lean}). With the vorticity supremum held at a constant $M$, the Beale--Kato--Majda integral $\int_0^T M\,dt=MT$ is finite on every finite interval, while the quadratic history $\int_0^T M^2\,dt=M^2T$ grows linearly in $T$. The two Lean statements, \texttt{bounded\_M\_keeps\_bkm\_finite} and \texttt{history\_can\_grow\_while\_bkm\_stays\_finite}, hold by definitional unfolding (\texttt{rfl}) of the model functions; they are arithmetic, not analysis of Navier--Stokes solutions. The point is only that linear growth of a history clock does not by itself indicate finite-time blowup. Nothing here proves or disproves Fefferman Statements (C) or (D).
\end{abstract}

\section{Scope, Scientific Honesty, and Non-Claim Boundary}
Worked illustration of why a diverging quadratic history clock on $[0,\infty)$ is not a finite-time BKM blowup proof; explicitly declines to certify Fefferman (C) or (D).

This repository is not a resolution of Fefferman (C) or (D).

A request to title a repository as a Clay C/D resolution, and to certify a zero-sorry blowup proof from the Section 10 argument, is declined. The Section 10 materials do not prove that the Beale--Kato--Majda integral diverges in finite time.

Blowup in the BKM sense is divergence of $\int\|\omega\|_\infty\,dt$. In the 2D and steady-Euler example $M$ is bounded, so $\int_0^T M\,dt=MT$ stays finite while $\int_0^T M^2\,dt$ grows. That is not a blowup proof.

Forced (C) and (D) are a different statement. An external claim exists in openai/NavierStokesAndEuler. This repository does not copy it and does not certify it.
