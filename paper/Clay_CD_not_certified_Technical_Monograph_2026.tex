\begin{abstract}
Standalone Lean 4 illustration (`lean/NotBlowup.lean`) proving `bounded\_M\_keeps\_bkm\_finite` and `history\_can\_grow\_while\_bkm\_stays\_finite`: a bounded vorticity supremum M keeps the Beale-Kato-Majda integral int\_0\textasciicircum{}T M dt = M T finite on every finite interval [0, T] even while the quadratic enstrophy history integral int\_0\textasciicircum{}T M\textasciicircum{}2 dt = M\textasciicircum{}2 T grows linearly. Consequently, linear growth of the history clock does not certify finite-time blowup or resolve Fefferman Statements (C) or (D).
\end{abstract}

\section{Scope, Scientific Honesty, and Non-Claim Boundary}
Worked illustration of why a diverging quadratic history clock on [0, infty) is not a finite-time BKM blowup proof; explicitly declines to certify Fefferman (C) or (D).

This repository is not a resolution of Fefferman (C) or (D).

A request to title a repository as a Clay C/D resolution, and to certify a zero-sorry blowup proof from the Section 10 argument, is declined. The Section 10 materials do not prove that the Beale–Kato–Majda integral diverges in finite time.

Blowup in the BKM sense is divergence of ∫ ‖ω‖\_∞ dt. The 2D and steady-Euler kill switch has M bounded, so ∫\_0\textasciicircum{}T M dt = M T stays finite, while ∫ M² grows. That is the opposite of a blowup proof.

Forced (C) and (D) are a different statement. An external claim exists in openai/NavierStokesAndEuler. This repository does not copy it and does not certify it.
